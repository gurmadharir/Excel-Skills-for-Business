# 📘 Course 3 – Intermediate II

### 🎯 Overview

Take your Excel abilities to the **intermediate level** and boost your productivity.  

Enhance your Excel expertise by learning how to **prevent errors**, apply **conditional logic**, **automate lookups**, **audit and protect workbooks**, and build **data models** and **forecasting spreadsheets** using **macros**.

---

### 🛡️ Module 1 – Data Validation

This module takes you through **creating** and **applying data validation**, as well as working with **formulas** in **data validation**. This is followed by **basic** and **advanced conditional formatting**.

**Key Lessons:**    

- **Create** and **apply data validation** in spreadsheets.  
- Work with **formulas** in **data validation**.  
- **Create** and use **drop-down lists**.  
- **Generate** **custom conditional formats**.

---

### ⚙️ Module 2 – Conditional Logic

Learn how to perform **logic tests**, apply **conditional operations**, and use **nested IF functions** to **evaluate** and **manipulate data** effectively.

**Key Lessons**    

- **Explain** the concept of **conditional logic** in formulas.  
- **Evaluate data** in a cell using **logical tests**.  
- Use **conditional operations** in functions (**IF**, **AND**, **OR**).  
- **Evaluate data** with **nested IF functions**.

---

### 🧩 Module 3 – Automating Lookups

Learn how to use **CHOOSE**, **VLOOKUP**, **INDEX**, **MATCH**, and other techniques to efficiently **find** and **display data** across multiple sources.

**Key Lessons**    

- Use the **VLOOKUP** function to **find** and **display** the contents of a cell.  
- Identify the use and requirements of the **range lookup** feature.  
- **Look up data** using the **INDEX** and **MATCH** functions.
  
---

### 📝 Module 4 – Formula Auditing and Protection

Learn how to **identify errors**, **trace precedents and dependents**, **resolve circular references**, and **safeguard your worksheets and workbooks** from potential issues.

**Key Lessons**    

- **Configure Formula Calculation Options**.  
- **Trace Precedents and Dependents**.  
- **Explain** how to **check for errors** in a spreadsheet.  
- **Protect workbooks and worksheets**.

---

### 📈 Module 5 – Data Modeling

Learn how to **model scenarios** based on **inputs**, **assumptions**, or **outcomes**, and leverage tools like **Goal Seek**, **Data Tables**, and **Scenario Manager** to build **robust**, **dynamic models**.

**Key Lessons**    

- **Model different scenarios** based on **input**, **assumptions**, and/or **outcomes**.  
- Use **Goal Seek** and **Solver** to investigate what **input parameters** produce a **desired outcome**.  
- Use **Data Tables** and **Scenario Manager**.

---

### 🎬 Module 6 – Recording Macros

Learn how to **create**, **edit**, and **manage macros**, and discover practical ways to **streamline tasks** and **increase efficiency**.

**Key Lessons**    

- **Identify** the uses of **macros** in Excel.  
- **Create basic macros** to **automate repetitive tasks**.  
- **Edit macros** to **extend their functionality**.  
- **Manage macros efficiently**.

---

### ✅ Module 7 – Final Assessment

Showcase your **mastery** by applying all the learned **tools** and **techniques** to **realistic business scenarios**.  

**Objectives:**

- **Integrate multiple Excel skills** in a **final project**.
